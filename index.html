Servo-derived browser engine / experimental

The engine behind Galileo Browser.

GalileoEngine is derived from Servo and developed to power Galileo Browser. We retain Servo’s Rust-based web-engine foundation while building the integration, compatibility work, desktop layer, and release discipline a complete browser requires.

Current work

Working software, narrow evidence, no public release.

Development builds and focused browser journeys are being verified now. That proves a foundation exists; it does not yet prove broad compatibility, production security, or everyday-browser readiness. Galileo Browser remains an in-development product.

The lineage

From upstream engine to user-facing browser.

These are related layers, not interchangeable names. Servo is the upstream foundation. GalileoEngine is the derived engine and integration layer. Galileo Browser is the product people will use.

What we add

A derived engine still has to earn its purpose.

These are the responsibilities that turn a web engine into a product. Each one has to be understood and tested before it becomes a promise.

01

Maintain the lineage

Keep the relationship with Servo explicit, make divergence intentional, and avoid a fork that cannot follow upstream progress.

02

Complete the engine surface

Turn focused engine capability into coherent navigation, storage, media, permissions, profiles, and desktop integration.

03

Qualify the browser

Compatibility, security, privacy, accessibility, stability, packaging, and updates need evidence before release claims.

What works today

Evidence, with the limits attached.

The project records narrow, repeatable results. A passing focused check is useful evidence, but it is not presented as broad browser support.
