Where funding would go

Specific work, reported clearly.

Financial support would be assigned to these project needs. A public budget and reporting cadence will be published before sponsorship opens.

01

Build & test infrastructure

Reliable build capacity, CI workloads, retained test evidence, and the services required to keep them reproducible.

02

Compatibility & quality

Focused browser testing, test hardware, platform coverage, and investigation of failures before claims expand.

03

Release foundations

Domains, distribution infrastructure, code-signing preparation, security review, and release documentation when the project reaches those gates.

04

Sustained engineering

Time for implementation, review, documentation, contributor support, and the less visible maintenance that keeps the project dependable.

Stewardship

Funding should strengthen the project, not distort it.

Organization first

The future Galileo organization will be the published sponsorship recipient. The accountable entity and payout route will be named before payments are accepted.

Visible reporting

Funding received, expense categories, and meaningful changes to the plan will be summarized publicly on a regular schedule.

No purchased influence

Sponsorship will not buy equity, feature priority, technical control, a release date, or a claim that the browser is more mature than the evidence shows.
