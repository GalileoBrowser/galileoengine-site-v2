Roadmap / evidence before claims

A browser needs a chain of proof.

GalileoEngine is organised around dependency and qualification: build the foundation, measure it, then move to the next promise. Nothing skips the gate in front of it.

The path

Current direction

The complete roadmap has many workstreams. This is the public view: the milestones that explain where the project is and what must become true next.

  1. Now 01

    Baseline & compatibility

    Establish reproducible builds, inventory the inherited shell, run focused checks, and record compatibility and quality evidence without overstating it.

    In progress
  2. Next 02

    Browser shell minimum

    Build the desktop surface that makes everyday browsing possible: navigation, tabs, windows, profiles, settings, storage, downloads, and recovery.

    Planned
  3. Then 03

    Security & compatibility

    Deepen isolation, privacy controls, standards coverage, real-site journeys, accessibility, graphics, media, networking, and automation.

    Gated
  4. Release 04

    Qualification

    Prove performance, stability, packaging, updates, crash recovery, support boundaries, and release readiness before an alpha or beta claim.

    Future
