The road / fork → release

From the fork to the first release.

The first three rows are dated focused milestones. Current rows run in parallel. Future rows stay open until their entry conditions are met.

  1. 01

    The fork

    26 July 2026 — Galileo starts where Servo ends: a 15.2-million-line snapshot, a new clock, a public repository. Everything after this commit counts as work.

    done · 26 Jul 2026
  2. 02

    Cloudflare Turnstile

    The official Turnstile canary passes in all three modes — always-pass, always-fail, forced-interactive — headless and headed, with the parity evidence refreshed on 6 Aug. Production challenges remain Cloudflare's decision; we do not fake fingerprints to pass them.

    done · 01 Aug 2026
  3. 03

    YouTube, working

    Media Source Extensions unblocked from day one, then qualified on 14 Aug: unattended YouTube playback, cold start, clock stalls, and replay — with diagnostics that survive the hard pages.

    done · 14 Aug 2026
  4. 04

    Baseline and product shell

    Browser chrome, tabs, profiles, private browsing, history, bookmarks, downloads, settings, and recovery are present at different levels. Most remain observed partial until their focused journeys are retained.

    now · in progress
  5. 05

    Security foundation

    Origin, network, cookie, navigation, and renderer IPC boundaries are active work. Process isolation, threat modelling, signing, updates, and the security response path remain release gates.

    now · open gate
  6. 06

    Extensions and ecosystem

    Manifest V2 and V3 entrypoints, permission review, content scripts, storage, and request blocking exist in partial form. The runtime is being split into reviewable modules; broad WebExtensions and uBlock Origin compatibility are not claimed.

    now · in progress
  7. 07

    Engineering pre-alpha package

    A Linux packaging path can produce a source-bound, stripped engineering archive for controlled testing. It remains unsigned, host-dependent, and unsuitable for public or security-sensitive use.

    tooling · bounded
  8. 08

    Public pre-alpha

    The first public build comes only after the selected compatibility, security, packaging, and update gates can be reproduced. It will still be for testing, not daily browsing.

    next · gates open
  9. 09

    Alpha and release qualification

    Broader Web Platform Tests, real-site journeys, accessibility, cross-platform packaging, performance budgets, crash recovery, security review, and signed updates must move from partial evidence to repeatable qualification.

    future
  10. 10

    Release

    Version 1.0 begins only when the release contract is satisfied and the browser can be maintained safely after launch.

    future
