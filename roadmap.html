GalileoEngine reports retained evidence, known limits, and the next technical gate. There is no percentage here because the project does not yet have a complete, stable denominator for browser readiness.

Development path

From a measured baseline to a qualified release.

The current phase is the foundation and browser baseline. Later stages remain gated until their entry conditions and evidence are available.

  1. 01Current

    Foundation & baseline

    Build, launch, inventory, and retain focused evidence.

  2. 02Focused proof

    Core browser journeys

    Rendering, navigation, storage, media, and shell behaviour.

  3. 03Next gate

    Compatibility & hardening

    Broader standards, sites, security, privacy, and accessibility.

  4. 04Gated

    Packaging & updates

    Install, update, rollback, recovery, and platform integration.

  5. 05Goal

    Public alpha

    A scoped release only after explicit qualification criteria pass.

The highlighted node shows the current project phase. “Focused proof” means selected paths have evidence; it does not mean the phase is complete.

The goal

A secure, accessible, maintainable engine for Galileo Browser.

The target is not simply to open more pages. GalileoEngine must make standards support, user safety, desktop reliability, and long-term maintenance measurable enough to support a real browser release.

01

Standards & interoperability

Broaden measurable web-platform support and real-site reliability.

02

Complete browser surface

Connect engine capability to coherent daily browsing workflows.

03

Release discipline

Require security, privacy, accessibility, stability, and delivery gates.

04

Healthy Servo lineage

Keep reusable improvements upstream-compatible whenever practical.
