How we work

AI increases the reach of a small team. It does not replace ownership.

We split work into bounded tasks, run the code, inspect the failures, and keep a record of what the result does not prove. That is the only way this pace is useful to anyone outside the room.

We are a European team, and the work is built in Europe. It is not European software: it should run for everyone, in their language, on hardware that answers to the person holding it — and it should stay open enough that no single company can put it in a box. The ambition is the method itself, reused on the next project that deserves to move.
