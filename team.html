How the team works

Fast work still needs a named owner.

We divide work into bounded tasks, reproduce failures, review changes, and record what each result does and does not prove. The process matters because a browser has to earn trust over time.

AI carries implementation volume. The team chooses the direction, checks the code, runs the evidence, and owns the decision to merge or release. That boundary is not optional.

Built in Europe

European roots. Open reach.

The team works from Europe, but the software is intended for everyone: different languages, different hardware, and users who should remain in control of their own browser.
