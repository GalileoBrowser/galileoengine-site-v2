How the team works

Small scopes. Clear evidence. Clean handoffs.

The project separates ownership and records what was actually tested. That keeps a large browser codebase reviewable and makes progress reproducible.

01

Explicit ownership

Each track has a bounded scope so collaborators can move independently without overwriting one another.

02

Evidence before claims

Builds, tests, and retained results determine status; a merged change alone is not a finished gate.

03

Upstream-quality work

General engine improvements are kept reusable and suitable for contribution back to Servo.

Work with us

Start with a measurable task.

Read the contribution workflow, choose a bounded issue, and define how the result will be verified.
